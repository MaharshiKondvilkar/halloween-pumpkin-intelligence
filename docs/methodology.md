## Included

- USDA-reported pumpkin production
- State-level statistics
- Historical annual comparisons
- Harvested acreage
- Yield or calculated productivity
- Geographic concentration
- Top-state comparisons

## Excluded

- Farm-level private information
- Retail pumpkin sales
- Consumer purchase behavior
- Halloween spending
- Weather causality
- Price forecasting
- Unverified commercial datasets
